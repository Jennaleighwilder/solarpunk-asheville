Editorial

On the necessity of beautiful resistance

There is a prevailing assumption that sustainability must be ugly, inconvenient, and ascetic. We reject this entirely. The systems we design are not compromises—they are upgrades. Solar arrays that double as public art. Battery walls you're proud to show off. Microgrids that strengthen neighborhoods instead of utilities that exploit them.

We work out of the Blue Ridge Mountains and install across Western North Carolina. The solarpunk look isn't a veneer on greenwashed products—it's what happens when energy systems respect both people and place.

Every project begins with a question: what would this place look like if it were allowed to thrive for ten thousand years? The answer is always more beautiful than the present.

"The most radical thing you can do is build something that outlasts the current paradigm."
— Punk Solar Solutions, 2026
